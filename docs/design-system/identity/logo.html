Four marks

Logo directions

The app has no mark today - a 6px accent dot stands in. Four directions, each shown as a lockup and at the two sizes that actually decide a logo.

Four marks · lockup, 20px, 16px, on both grounds
Lanes
omp-web
omp-web
Parallel sessions at different depths. Says what the app is for - many concurrent agents - rather than what it runs on. Reads at 16px and doubles as a status motif: bar heights could track real session states.
Orbit
omp-web
omp-web
A controller with satellites: mission control made literal. Most distinctive, most likely to be mistaken for a generic AI or atom logo, and the weakest at 16px.
Caret
omp-web
omp-web
A prompt in a pane. Instantly legible as a terminal tool and the safest choice, but it is the logo half the developer-tool category already uses.
Panes
omp-web
omp-web
Four tiles at different weights - tmux panes, or sessions in a grid. Calm and structural; also the most anonymous, and close to several existing app icons.

Recommendation: Lanes. It is the only one of the four that means something specific to this app, it survives 16px, it needs no gradient or detail to read, and it extends: the same four bars can carry live status on the tab favicon. Orbit is the more beautiful mark and the worse logo.

Where a mark has to work

Sidebar brand at 16px (today: a bare 6px accent dot before the wordmark) · browser tab favicon at 16px · PWA icon at 192 and 512, which is where the current icon.svg lives · the iOS home-screen tile, where it sits on the user's wallpaper and needs its own padded ground.

None of these are implemented yet. This card is the choice, not the change.
